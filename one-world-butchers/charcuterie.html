How it's made

Whole-muscle first

We break primals in the shop, so the trim that goes into sausage is the same meat that goes in the case.

Natural casings

Hog and sheep casings, hand-linked. No fillers, no phosphates.

Small batches

A few kilos per recipe per run. If it's sold out, it's back in a day or two.
