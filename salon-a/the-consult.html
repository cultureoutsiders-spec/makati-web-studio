The consult

Written down, kept on file

Korean salons treat the consultation as part of the cut, not small talk before it. Yours is written on a sheet and kept, so the salon remembers your hair even if you do not. The example below is a placeholder for the demo.

Consult sheet · 0219

What we note

Hair type
Fine, slight wave, tends to flatten by midday
Density
Medium
Condition
Ends slightly damaged from old colour
Scalp
Normal, no sensitivity noted
Lifestyle
Air-dries most days, straightener twice a week
Reference
Two saved photos: layered bob, curtain fringe

Plan this visit: 4cm off, internal layers for movement, fringe cut dry to check the face shape. Bond treatment recommended; hold colour until the ends recover. Home routine: two products, shown at the chair.

Why it matters

The sheet means your second visit is faster and the cut is repeatable. A different stylist can pick it up. And if you want to change something, there is a written record of what you had before.

First visit

Allow 15 minutes extra. Bring reference photos if you have them; a photo of a past cut you liked is just as useful.

Your copy

Ask and we send you a photo of your sheet after the appointment.
