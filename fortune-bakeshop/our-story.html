Our story

Baking in Makati since 1974

One shop, one recipe, and a stubborn habit of doing it the same way. The details below are placeholder history for the demo.

1974

The first shop

Fortune Bakeshop opens as a single neighbourhood panaderia, selling pandesal, monay and hopia to the streets around it.

1990s

A second, then a third

Word travels. A few more small branches open around Makati, each one running the same recipes off the same schedule.

2000s

The cake case

Chiffon cakes, brazo and greeting cakes join the counter — the shop becomes the place the barangay orders birthdays from.

Today

Still 4 AM

A handful of branches, family-run, mixing before dawn so the first tray of pandesal is out by six. No franchise, no factory.

What hasn't changed

Real butter in the ensaymada. Pandesal small and soft, not puffed up with air. Prices a neighbour can afford every morning. That's the whole business.
