## How the check was done

Same test as batches 1–4: if every result is a Facebook/Instagram page, a TikTok, a
GrabFood/FoodPanda listing, a directory entry, or a **third-party booking widget**
(SimplyBook, Booking.page, Welns, about.me, Linktree), and there is **no own-domain website**,
it's a prospect. Each one below was Googled by name; none has a real "Website" button pointing at
its own domain.

re-research them: Tiño Suits (`tinosuits.com`), Tilde Bakery & Kitchen (`tildebakery.com`),

## The five

### 1. Blue Door 304 — hidden speakeasy cocktail bar
- **Where:** 3rd Floor, Matheus Building, Gen. Luna St., Barangay Poblacion, Makati
- **Presence:** Instagram `@thebluedoor304` + Facebook + an `about.me/bluedoor304` link-in-bio
  page. **No website.** Soft-launched September 2025 — new.
- **Contact:** Facebook / Instagram DM. No public phone; private bookings arranged by DM.
  Open Tue–Thu & Sun 6pm–1am, Fri–Sat 6pm–2am.
- **Pitch angle:** the whole premise is "you have to find it" — three knocks on a bronze door
  knocker, a window check. That's a perfect brief for a site: a moody one-pager that gives just
  enough (the block, the hours, a private-booking form) without breaking the mystery. Right now a
  first-timer has to piece it together from three different blog write-ups.
- **Template:** a dark, atmospheric single-scroll landing + a private-event / table-request form
  (closest to Ambient's "what's on" hero, but moodier and smaller).

### 2. MSM Wells Photo — analog film photo lab
- **Where:** 67 Epifanio de los Santos Ave (South Super Highway), Makati City
- **Presence:** Instagram `@msmwellsphoto` + Facebook. Directory listings only. **No website.**
  A real working lab — C-41, E-6, black & white, cross-processing, plus custom printing and
  framing.
- **Contact:** +63 2 8845 1483 · Facebook / Instagram DM. "Check our Facebook for pricing."
- **Pitch angle:** film shooters plan around turnaround, formats and prices, and right now that
  all lives in scattered IG captions and DMs. A site with a clear services + film-format + price
  table, a turnaround tracker, and a drop-off / mail-in form turns "DM us to ask" into booked
  jobs. The lab aesthetic (contact sheets, frame numbers, chemical timers) is a gift of a brief.
- **Template:** services + spec table + a "how a roll moves through the lab" flow + drop-off
  form (adapts the Easyshare "order desk" pattern to a warmer, analog register).

### 3. One World Butchers — butcher, deli & charcuterie
- **Where:** 8491 Kalayaan Avenue corner Matilde Street, Barangay Poblacion, Makati
- **Presence:** Facebook `/oneworldbutchers` + FoodPanda for delivery. **No own website.**
  A butchery + deli + BBQ restaurant in one, known for a wide range of house-made artisanal
  sausages.
- **Contact:** 0917 823 6631 · Facebook Messenger. Open Sun & Tue–Wed 11am–10pm, Thu–Sat
  11am–12am.
- **Pitch angle:** a butcher lives on its counter list — cuts, sausages, what's in today, what to
  pre-order for a party. None of that fits a Facebook feed. A site with the daily case, the
  sausage range, whole-animal / bulk pre-orders and a "book the pit" enquiry does the selling the
  feed can't. The butcher-paper / cut-chart / chalk-price aesthetic is strong and unused.
- **Template:** product board + spec/price tables + bulk-order enquiry form (F&B retail — related
  to Fortune's "bread board" receipt idea but a very different trade and look).

### 4. Gray Matter Showroom — curated archive / grail fashion showroom
- **Where:** 5141 Zamora St., Barangay Poblacion, Makati (1pm–8pm, **by appointment**)
- **Presence:** Instagram `@graymmatterr_` only. **No website.** Sells coveted, exclusive pieces
  — streetwear to avant-garde to Americana — from an array of designers and brands.
- **Contact:** Instagram DM only.
- **Pitch angle:** an appointment-only archive showroom is exactly the kind of business that
  benefits from a real site — a proper lookbook/inventory grid, the appointment mechanic, the
  provenance/condition notes per piece, a "what we're looking to buy" page. Right now it's a
  grid of IG posts and a DM.
- **Template:** a lean editorial catalogue — inventory grid with piece detail + an
  appointment-request form (adapts the Yan yan soo "flower table" filter grid to fashion, drier
  and more gallery-like).

### 5. Kombat Sports Kalayaan (KSK) — multi-discipline martial-arts academy
- **Where:** 8471 Kalayaan Ave corner Fermina St., Makati (Poblacion end)
- **Presence:** Instagram `@kombatsportskalayaan` (~9K) + Facebook. Booking runs through
  third-party widgets (SimplyBook, Booking.page) — **no own website.** Boxing, Muay Thai, MMA,
  No-Gi BJJ, circuit training, yoga and taekwondo; also runs a fight team / promotes cards.
- **Contact:** +63 961 022 6461 · Instagram / Facebook DM.
- **Pitch angle:** a serious multi-discipline academy with a fight team, and the class schedule,
  the coach roster, the trial-class offer and the membership tiers all live in IG highlights and
  a bare SimplyBook page. A real site — weekly timetable, coaches, "your first class", pricing,
  trial-booking form — is a straight upgrade. (Distinct from COREX in batch 1, which is a
  boxing + HYROX *performance* gym; KSK is a traditional academy.)
- **Template:** schedule grid + coaches + pricing + free-trial booking (the COREX pattern, but a
  grittier dojo identity — chalk, tape, timer bells).

## How to work this list

- **Email-first:** none of the five publish an email. All are **DM / call-first** (like COREX and
  most of batches 3–4). Grab an email off each Facebook "About" tab first if there is one.
- **Build order that reuses work:** Easyshare "order desk" → MSM Wells; Fortune "product board" →
  One World Butchers; COREX schedule pattern → KSK; Yan yan soo filter grid → Gray Matter;
  Ambient "what's on" (moodier) → Blue Door 304.
