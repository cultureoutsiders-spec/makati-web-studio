How it works

Consult first, colour second

Colour is a considered job. We do not mix anything until you have a price, a timeline and an honest read on what your hair can take. The steps below are a placeholder for the demo.

The appointment

  1. Consultation. Bring reference photos. We assess your hair's history and condition, agree the target, and give you a firm price and how long it will take. A patch test is done here if you are new to a colour type.
  2. Booking the sitting. Fashion colour and correction need a long slot; we book it when the patch test is clear (48 hours).
  3. The sitting. Lightening, then colour, then a gloss and a bond treatment. You will be in the chair for the time quoted. Bring a charger and something to do.
  4. The finish. Blow-dried so you see the real result, with a note of the exact formula on file for next time.
  5. Aftercare. A short list of what to use and what to avoid, plus when to come back for a gloss.

Keeping colour right

Sulphate-free shampoo, cooler water, heat protectant, and a purple or blue toning wash once a week for blondes and ash tones. A gloss every 4–6 weeks for fashion shades.

What we will not do

Push a lift your hair cannot survive in one visit, or promise a platinum result from a dark box dye in one sitting. We would rather split it or say no.
