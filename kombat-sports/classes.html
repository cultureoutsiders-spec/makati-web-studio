Classes

Pick a discipline

Every class starts with a warm-up and technique, then drilling, then (for the right levels) sparring or rolling. Beginners are never thrown in. Schedule below is placeholder for the demo.

Boxing

Hands first

Stance, footwork, the jab, defence, then combinations on pads and bags. Fundamentals class daily; a sparring class twice a week for those cleared for it.

  • Fundamentals — no experience needed
  • Technical — 3+ months in
  • Sparring — coach's approval

Muay Thai

Eight points

Punches, elbows, knees, kicks and the clinch. Heavy on pad rounds and partner drills. All-levels class most evenings.

  • All-levels — scaled per person
  • Clinch & sparring — intermediate up

No-Gi BJJ

Ground game

Positional control, escapes, submissions — no gi, so it's grip-light and fast. Live rolling from your first month, at your pace.

  • Fundamentals — positions & escapes
  • Advanced — submission chains, competition prep
  • Open mat — weekends

MMA

All of it

Striking into takedowns into ground-and-pound and submissions. You'll want a few months of striking and grappling before this one.

  • Prereq — some boxing/Muay Thai + some BJJ
  • Fight-team track by invitation
