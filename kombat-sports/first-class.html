Your first class

Round one

You will not spar on day one. You'll warm up, learn a few things slowly, hit some pads and leave tired. Here's the detail.

Bring

  • Shorts or leggings, a t-shirt, and a towel
  • Water
  • Hand wraps if you have them — we sell them, and lend gloves
  • For BJJ: a rashguard and board shorts (no pockets/zips)

The hour

  • Arrive 15 min early — sign the waiver, quick tour
  • Warm-up with the class
  • Technique, broken down, done slow with a partner
  • Drilling — the same move, many reps
  • Pads or positional rounds at your level
  • Cool down, questions, done

Common worries

"I'm not fit enough"

Nobody is at first. The class builds it. Rest when you need to; no one's watching.

"I'll get hurt"

Beginner classes don't spar. When you do, it's supervised, geared and controlled.

"Everyone's better than me"

They were all new once and they know it. It's the least ego-heavy room you'll train in.
