How it works

Four steps, then a week of meals

  1. 1 · Reserve

    Message us by Friday for the following week. Tell us your address, start day, and any allergies or dishes to skip.

  2. 2 · We plan

    We confirm the week’s rotation and the delivery window. Pay by GCash or bank transfer to lock it in.

  3. 3 · Delivered

    Two meals a day arrive together, chilled, labelled, ready to reheat. Free within our delivery zones for whole-week plans.

  4. 4 · Repeat

    Like it? Roll it over. Regulars just message “same again” and we take it from there.
