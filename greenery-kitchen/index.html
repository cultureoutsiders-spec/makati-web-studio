what this isA home kitchen that went vegan and never looked back

Greenery Kitchen started in 2007 as home-cooked lunches for a small meditation community. Almost twenty years on it’s a proper little restaurant on E. Pascua Street — same approach, bigger pans. Everything is 100% plant-based: no meat, no fish, no shortcuts.
