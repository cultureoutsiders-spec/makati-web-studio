The method

Xiamen-style, worked to a map

Chinese reflexology treats the foot as a map of the body. Firm, specific pressure on a reflex point is used to work the area it corresponds to. The Xiamen style is on the firmer end. The notes below are a general placeholder for the demo, not medical advice.

Your first visit

  1. A short talk at the chair: what you do, how you sleep, where you carry tension, anything a therapist should avoid.
  2. A warm herbal soak while the therapist reads your feet.
  3. The work: thumb and knuckle pressure along the reflex lines, section by section. Tight points get more time.
  4. Tell the therapist to ease off or go deeper at any point. Firm should be "good sore", not sharp pain.
  5. Tea and a few minutes to sit. Drink water through the day.

Good for

Tired, aching feet, long days standing or in heels, jet lag, screen headaches, general tension and winding down. Regular sessions as maintenance.

Check with us first

Pregnancy, recent surgery, injuries, blood-thinning medication, or any circulatory condition. We adjust pressure and areas, or suggest you clear it with a doctor.

Reflexology is a complementary therapy for relaxation and comfort. It is not a treatment for illness and does not replace medical care.
