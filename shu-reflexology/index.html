What to expect

The soak

Every foot session opens with a warm herbal soak while the therapist reads your feet and asks where you hold tension.

The work

Firm, specific thumb and knuckle pressure along the reflex lines. Tell the therapist to ease off or dig in; it is your session.

After

Warm tea, a few minutes to sit. Drink water for the rest of the day. Bruising on tight spots is normal and fades fast.
