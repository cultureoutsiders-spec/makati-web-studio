What's a koffle?

Coffee's the drink. Koffle's the sandwich.

A crisp, golden waffle folded around something good — egg and cheese in the morning, ham or mushroom the rest of the day, ice cream if it's that kind of afternoon. Made to order, eaten warm, best with a flat white.
