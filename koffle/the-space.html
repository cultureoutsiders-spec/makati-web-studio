The space

Small on purpose

Koffle is one room. Warm wood, a lot of plants, a window that fogs up when it rains. About a dozen seats. We kept it small so it always feels like someone's living room.

The idea

Two things we love — a properly made coffee and a fresh, crisp waffle — folded into one little place. The name's the whole story: Koffle = coffee + waffle.

We roast with a local roaster and grind per cup. The waffles are made to order on a cast iron, so a koffle takes a few minutes — that's the deal.

good things, slowly.
