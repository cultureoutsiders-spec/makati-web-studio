Location & getting around

Kalayaan Avenue,
corner Mercado

On the edge where Poblacion's nightlife meets the Makati business district. Walk one way for bars and food, the other for offices and Greenbelt.

On foot or a short ride

Distances

Getting from the airport

Grab or the airport coupon taxi to Kalayaan Avenue, roughly 25 to 35 minutes depending on traffic.

Around Makati

Everything in the CBD is a short Grab or a 10 to 15 minute walk. Jeepneys and buses run along Kalayaan and Makati Avenue.

Parking

Limited building parking, arrange in advance at reception. Street and pay parking nearby.
