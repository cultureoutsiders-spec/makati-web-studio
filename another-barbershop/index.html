What you get

A booking that holds

Pick a barber and a time. If you are a regular, we keep your usual on a card so you do not have to explain it every visit.

No rush

One chair per slot. You are not being cycled through while three people wait behind you.

The shelf

Pomade, clay, tonic, blades and a few local brands. Whatever went on your hair, you can buy.

The room

Four chairs, a shelf, a bench out front

Nothing fancy. Good light, sharp tools, a playlist that is not too loud, and barbers who have been cutting here long enough to know the block.
