## How to work this list

- **Email-first (send like batch 1):** Resabal Dental, Ambient Bar & Restaurant, Little Gee Plants — plus any of the alternates with a gmail.
- **DM-first (like COREX):** Greenery Kitchen, Kangnam Beauty — both are high-value (big audiences, strong reviews), worth the extra step of a Facebook/Instagram message.
- Build order that reuses work: **clinic template** (Resabal → also serves Kangnam) and **small F&B template** (Ambient, Greenery, Little Gee) are each built once and reused.
- Verify web presence yourself before pitching, and grab the email off the Facebook "About" tab where this list only has a phone.
