The small-room situation

  • Seats about ten. On weekends there's often a short wait — pre-order and we'll have your bowl ready to take away or on the counter when a seat opens.
  • Every bowl is made to order, so drinks take a few minutes. That's the point.
  • Quiet space — it's a tea room, not a co-working spot. Calls outside, please.
  • Retail tins of matcha and hojicha to take home.
