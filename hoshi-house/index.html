The ritual

Sift

Matcha clumps. We sift every bowl so the whisk has a chance.

Water, not boiling

70–80°C. Boiling water scorches matcha and makes it bitter.

Whisk

Bamboo chasen, quick W-strokes, until there's a fine even foam.

Drink it soon

Matcha separates. It's at its best in the first few minutes — no rush, but don't leave it.
