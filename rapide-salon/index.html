How a car moves through the bay

Four stages, in order

Detailing isn't one job — it's a sequence. Skip a step and the next one traps the dirt under it. Here's the order we work in.

STAGE 01

Wash & decon

Foam, hand wash, iron fallout and tar removal, clay. The paint is physically clean before anything touches it.

STAGE 02

Correction

Machine polishing to cut swirls, wash marks and light scratches. One or two stages depending on the paint.

STAGE 03

Coating

Panel wipe, then the ceramic layer applied by hand, section by section, and levelled.

STAGE 04

Cure & check

Rest under cover, final inspection under lights, tint checked, keys back with care notes.
