The process

What actually happens in the bay

Every job runs the same order. The difference between packages is how far down the list we go and how long the protection lasts — not whether a step gets skipped.

STAGE 01 — WASH & DECONTAMINATION

Get the paint physically clean

Pre-rinse and foam to lift loose grit, then a two-bucket hand wash. Wheels, arches and shuts are done separately. Then decontamination: an iron-fallout remover for the bonded brake dust, a tar solvent, and a clay bar to pull out what's still stuck in the clearcoat. Nothing abrasive touches the paint until it's genuinely clean.

  • Foam pre-wash + two-bucket contact wash
  • Iron fallout & tar removal
  • Clay decontamination, rinsed and dried
STAGE 02 — PAINT CORRECTION

Cut the swirls and marks

Under proper lighting we assess the paint and take readings. Machine polishing then removes wash marks, swirls and light scratches — one stage for newer paint, two for heavier defects. This is where the depth comes from; a coating over uncorrected paint just locks the swirls in.

  • Paint inspection under LED / swirl light
  • 1-stage (gloss) or 2-stage (defect removal) polish
  • Panel-wipe to remove all polishing oils
STAGE 03 — COATING

Lay the ceramic layer

Applied by hand, one section at a time, then levelled before it flashes. The coating bonds to the clearcoat and gives you the slick, beading surface that makes washing easier and holds gloss for years. Tint, if you're having it, goes on around this stage in a dust-controlled area.

  • Final panel wipe
  • Ceramic applied & levelled section by section
  • Window tint fitted & heat-shrunk (optional)
STAGE 04 — CURE & FINAL CHECK

Rest, inspect, hand back

The car sits under cover while the coating cures — this is why longer packages need an overnight. Before pickup we go over every panel under lights, check the tint edges, and give you the care notes: no wash for seven days, and how to keep it looking like this.

  • Covered cure (a few hours to overnight)
  • Panel-by-panel inspection under lighting
  • Written aftercare + first maintenance wash guidance

Common questions

Can I wait for it?

A Reset wash you can wait for (~4 hrs). Correction and coating packages need the car overnight or across two days.

Do you do all sizes?

Sedan to SUV and pickups. Larger vehicles add to the correction time and the quote.

Is a quote fixed?

The package price is a starting point. We confirm the final figure after seeing the paint — no surprises after that.
