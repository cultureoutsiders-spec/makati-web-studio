Little Gee Plants

Plants that survive
a real apartment

We stock houseplants that hold up in Metro Manila condos and offices — the light is what it is, the aircon is drying, and life is busy. Come in, tell us your space, and we'll point you at something that'll live.

Usually in stock

What we carry

Easy starters

Pothos, ZZ, snake plant, aglaonema. Low light, low fuss, hard to kill.

Statement plants

Monstera, birds of paradise, rubber plant, fiddle-leaf — for a bright corner and a bit of commitment.

For the brave

Calatheas, ferns, alocasias. Beautiful, dramatic, and honest about needing attention.

Desk-sized

Small succulents, peperomia, pilea — for a windowsill or an office desk.

Pots & soil

Nursery pots, ceramic and terracotta, potting mix, drainage and basic tools.

Gifting

A potted plant with a card beats a bouquet that dies in a week. We'll wrap it.

Stock changes with what's healthy and available. Message us to check before a special trip.

A care card, in plain terms

ZZ Plant

Light
Low to bright indirect
Water
Every 2–3 weeks, less in the rainy season
Watch for
Yellow leaves = overwatered

Pothos

Light
Anything but direct sun
Water
When the top inch is dry
Watch for
Leggy vines = needs more light

Snake Plant

Light
Low to bright, tolerant
Water
Every 3–4 weeks, soak then dry
Watch for
Mushy base = too much water
