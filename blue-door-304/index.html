Inside

One room, twelve seats, a short list

How to get in

Find the building

Matheus Building on Gen. Luna Street, Poblacion. No sign. Take the stairs to the third floor.

Knock — three times

On the bronze knocker. A small window opens for a look.

Come in if there's room

If the room's full you can leave a number and we'll message when a seat opens. Walk-ins only — no reservations for the bar itself.
