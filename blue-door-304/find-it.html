Find it

Gen. Luna Street. Third floor. Blue door.

There's no sign and we like it that way. Here's exactly how to get in.

Get to the block

Gen. Luna Street, Barangay Poblacion, Makati — the stretch between Kalayaan and Enriquez. Matheus Building. Look for the door beside [landmark], not the one with the tarpaulin.

Third floor

Stairs at the side. No lift. Keep going up past the second-floor tenant.

The blue door

Unmarked except for a small brass "304" plate. Bronze knocker in the middle.

Knock three times

A window opens for a look. If there's room, you're in. If not, leave a number and we'll message when a seat frees up.

Before you come

Hours

Tue–Thu & Sun, 6pm–1am. Fri–Sat, 6pm–2am. Closed Monday.

Walk-in only

No reservations for the bar itself. Come early on weekends; the room is small.

House rules

Phones down where you can. No standing orders of shots. Be kind to the person at the window.
