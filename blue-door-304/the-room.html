The room

Small on purpose

Low ceiling, low light, a marble bar and a handful of stools and two-tops. It fills up and it empties out; when it's full, it's full. That's the whole design.

Capacity

About 12 seated at the bar and the two-tops, a few more standing. For a private night we cap it around 20 so it still feels like the room it is.

Sound

Records, kept low enough to talk over. No DJ, no dance floor, no requests.

Booking it out

Take the whole room

Birthdays, small work things, a night with friends. We'll set a minimum spend based on the date and headcount, and the bartenders will build a short menu around what your group drinks.
