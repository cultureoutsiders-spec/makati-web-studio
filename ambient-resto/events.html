    The space

    What you get

    The room

    Semi-private or full-venue hire. Comfortable for around 40 seated, more standing. Your own entrance flow for bigger nights.

    Sound & stage

    House PA, mics, a small stage, and lighting for programs. Bring your band or use our house act.

    Food & bar

    Set menu, buffet or platters. Drinks by package or consumption. Invoicing and official receipts available for companies.

    Coordination

    We line up with your host, stylist or supplier and run the timeline on the night so you can enjoy it.

    Timing

    Standard 3-hour block, extendable. Daytime slots available for corporate sessions.

    Parking

    Bautista Street has its own parking; we can reserve spaces for a fee on request.
