Vet clinic

A clinic next to the grooming room

Routine care and minor procedures, in the same building — so anything spotted during a groom or a stay gets seen without another trip across town. Placeholder detail for the demo.

Services

  • Wellness check-ups & senior screening
  • Vaccinations & deworming, with reminders
  • Skin, ear and eye consults
  • Dental cleaning & extractions
  • Minor surgery & wound care
  • Microchipping & travel certificates
  • Nutrition & weight-management advice

When to come in

  • Not eating for more than a day
  • Vomiting or diarrhoea that isn't settling
  • Limping, or a lump you haven't seen before
  • Scratching, head-shaking, or a smelly ear
  • Anything the groomer flagged on the report card
