How we handle it

Slow with nervous pets

First visits go at the pet's pace. If it's too much, we stop and reschedule — no forcing.

No cage dryers

Hand-dried, always supervised. Never left under a heat box.

Vet in the building

Anything the groomer spots — lumps, ears, skin — can be looked at the same day.
