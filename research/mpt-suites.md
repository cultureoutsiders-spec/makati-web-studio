## Building amenities (Makati Prime Tower)
Swimming pool · gym · spa · beauty centre · 24h reception. Note reviews are candid that the **rooms
themselves are basic** ("spartan and simple") — the draw is the building facilities, the price and
the location, not luxury fit-out. Pitch honestly on those.

## Location
3204A Kalayaan Avenue corner Mercado, Poblacion — the Makati Ave / P. Burgos edge. Immediate
neighbours (all ~0.1 km): I'M Hotel Makati, City Garden Grand Hotel, St Giles Makati, Sunette Tower.
Short walk / ride to Ayala Avenue, Greenbelt and the Poblacion bar strip.

- **Consolidate the mess:** right now the tower is sold under half a dozen operator names across OTAs.
  A single clean site on the domain they already own (makatiprimetowersuites.com) gives them a
  direct-booking channel and one identity.
- Real building facilities (pool, gym, spa) at a genuine budget price.
- Do **not** headline a star rating — OTA scores are mixed/low. Lead on location, facilities, value,
  suite sizes for groups, and direct booking.
