# Prospects — batch 4

Five more Makati businesses with no website of their own. **Method for this batch:** leads pulled
from **Google Maps** (category searches around Poblacion / San Antonio Village / Legazpi Village),
then each one **cross-checked on Facebook / Instagram** for a working website link. Researched
2026-09-09.

**STATUS: candidates only — no demo sites built yet.** Same offer applies: PHP 25,000 one-time,
no retainer (first 5 paying clients), then PHP 35,000.

## How the check was done

1. **Google Maps** — search a category, read each listing. Maps shows a "Website" line in the
   result when the business has one; absence of that line is the first signal.
2. **Facebook / Instagram** — for each no-website listing, open their FB page and IG profile and
   look for an own-domain link in the bio / "About" / "Contact us". A Linktree, a Google
   `business.site` auto-page, a GrabFood/Booksy link, or "DM us to book" does **not** count as a
   website.
3. Only businesses that came up clean on both are on the main list below.

**Disqualified on checking (they have a real site):** The Auto Atelier, R&R Auto Detail, Full
Torque Carwash (all auto-detailing with sites); Payapa Wellness, Well Being Nature Spa, Wellmana,
Celestia (spas with sites); Small Piece of Paper Printing; MECF Enterprises.

  responsive to DMs based on their post history.

  Insular Bakery Makati has a site separate from its Canadian namesake.
- **Build order that reuses work:** COREX (services + pricing + booking) → Rapide + SHALA;
  Resabal (clinic) → MOOI; Artina/Ambient enquiry form → Easyshare; Greenery "since 2007" →
  Insular Bakery.
