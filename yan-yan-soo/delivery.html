Good to know

  • We photograph every arrangement before it leaves.
  • Recipient not home? The rider calls you; we can leave it with a guard or neighbour with your OK.
  • Wrong address or refused delivery is re-delivered at a second fee.
  • Payment by GCash or bank transfer confirms the slot.
  • Anonymous sender? Just say so — we leave your name off the card.

Questions

Before you order

Can I order for a specific hour?

We aim for a 2–3 hour window, not an exact time. For something time-critical (a proposal, a stage moment) tell us and we'll prioritise it.

Do the flowers look like the photos?

Close — flowers are seasonal, so a stem may be swapped for something similar. We message you the finished piece first.

Provincial delivery?

Selected areas via courier, next-day, with a heat-safe pack. Ask us for a quote.

Corporate & standing orders?

Weekly lobby flowers, event pieces, bulk giveaways — message us and we'll set up a running arrangement.
