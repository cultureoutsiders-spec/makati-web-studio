A short guide

What to send, and what to say

Not sure which arrangement fits, or stuck on the card message? Here's how we'd steer you. Tell us the occasion when you order and we'll suggest options in your budget.

Birthday

Bright and personal beats formal. Their favourite colour, or a mixed bouquet with something unexpected. Card: keep it warm and specific — one real memory lands better than “happy birthday”.

Anniversary & romance

Roses still say it clearest — a dozen, or two if it's a big year. Ask us to match a colour to the first bouquet you ever gave. Card: short, sincere, no jokes.

Sympathy

White or soft-toned, nothing loud. A standing arrangement or wreath for a wake; a smaller posy for the home. Card: “With deepest sympathy — [name]” is enough. We can print the sash.

Congratulations

Sunflowers, a money bouquet for graduations, or a grand standing arrangement for an opening or promotion. Card: name the achievement — “On passing the boards” beats “Congrats”.

Get well

Cheerful but low-fuss — something that fits a bedside table, or a fruit-and-flower basket. Avoid heavy scent for hospital rooms. Card: light and brief.

Just because

The best reason there is. Tulips, a seasonal mix, or “surprise me” — tell us the person and we'll build something. Card: “Saw these and thought of you.”
