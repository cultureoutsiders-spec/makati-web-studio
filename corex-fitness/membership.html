Before you ask

Common questions

Do I need to be fit to start?

No. Every class scales. Tell the coach it's your first session and they'll adjust the round for you.

What do I bring?

Water, a towel, training clothes. Hand wraps for boxing/Muay Thai if you have them — gloves can be borrowed.

Can I freeze a package?

Packages have a validity window. Message us before it lapses and we'll work something out.

Is there parking?

The A&W Building is on Makati Avenue with pay parking nearby; most members walk or ride in.
