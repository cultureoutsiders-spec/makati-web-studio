01 — Service menu

What the bay handles

Periodic maintenance (PMS)

Scheduled service intervals — oil and filters, valve checks, chain and sprocket, brake inspection, road test.

Minor service

Oil change, brake pads, chain adjust, bulb and battery, general check-over before a long ride.

Major service

Deeper interval work — coolant, brake fluid, fork oil, throttle bodies, spark plugs, full inspection.

Diagnostics

Warning lights, running faults, electrical gremlins — traced and reported before any work is authorised.

Brakes & suspension

Pad and disc replacement, bleeds, fork seals, preload and sag setup.

Parts fitting

Exhausts, sliders, luggage, lighting and accessories — fitted properly, torqued to spec.

02 — Parts counter

Genuine parts, or we order them in

Filters, pads, discs, chains, sprockets, fairings, levers, consumables and gear for Honda, Kawasaki and Daytona. If it's not on the shelf, tell us the model and year and we'll source the genuine part.

OEM only · fitment checked · trade enquiries welcome
