Since 2001

Same address.
Same people.
Two decades.

851 Bike Park opened on J.P. Rizal Street in 2001, back when the big-bike scene in Metro Manila was a fraction of its size. We're still on that corner, still run by the same family, and still the shop other riders point you to for genuine parts and honest service.

01 — What hasn't changed

You talk to someone who rides

No call-centre script, no rotating sales floor. The person who quotes your service is the person who has seen a hundred of your model come through the bay. That continuity is the whole point — it's why people have trusted us with pre-owned purchases for twenty years.
