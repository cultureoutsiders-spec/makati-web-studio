Natural wine

A short, honest primer

You don't need this to enjoy the bar — the staff will pour you the right thing. But if you're curious, here's the gist.

What "low-intervention" means

Grapes farmed without synthetic chemicals, fermented with the yeast that's already on them, and bottled with little or nothing added — especially very little sulphur. Nothing taken out, nothing put in to make it look or taste a certain way. What you get is a wine that tastes of a place and a year, for better and sometimes for worse.

Why it's cloudy / fizzy / funky

  • Cloudy — unfiltered. The haze is grape and yeast solids. Harmless, often delicious.
  • Fizzy — pét-nat: bottled before fermentation finished, so it carbonates itself.
  • Funky on opening — a bit of reduction or a wild note that usually blows off in the glass. Give it a minute.
  • "Orange" — white grapes fermented on their skins, like a red. Tannic, savoury, food-friendly.

How to order if you're new

Tell the bar two things: something you normally drink, and how adventurous you're feeling. They'll pour a taste before you commit. There's no wrong pick — and if something isn't for you, say so and they'll swap it.
