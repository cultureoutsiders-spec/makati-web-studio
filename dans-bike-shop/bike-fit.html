Bike fit

Set up so it doesn't hurt

About 90 minutes, on your bike, with your shoes. You leave with the adjustments made and a fit sheet you can take to any bike. Placeholder detail for the demo.

Interview

Riding goals, hours per week, current aches, past injuries, flexibility. What "comfortable" means for you.

Baseline measurements

Saddle height & setback, reach, drop, crank length, cleat position — recorded before anything changes.

On the trainer

Pedalling under load while we watch from the side and front. Small changes, one at a time, feel checked after each.

Contact points

Saddle, bars, cleats. Swap parts if the current ones are fighting you — you're not obliged to buy.

Fit sheet

Every number written down, plus notes. Bring it back for a free tweak within a month if something's off.
