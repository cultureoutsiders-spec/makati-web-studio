The standing slot

Book once. Keep it.

A standing appointment is a recurring booking: same chair, same barber, same time, on a cadence you set. You stop thinking about haircuts. Details below are placeholders for the demo.

  1. Set a cadence. Every 2, 3 or 4 weeks. Pick a day and a time that works long-term.
  2. We book the run. The next six visits go in the calendar at once, with your barber and chair held.
  3. A nudge before each one. A message two days out. Reply to move it; no charge, no drama.
  4. Your cut is on file. Guard numbers, parting, how you like the beard. Any barber can pick it up if yours is away.
  5. Every fifth cut is on us. The shop covers it. It resets and runs again.
