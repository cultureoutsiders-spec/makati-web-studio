The name

Yes, it's a movie reference

And no, there's no theme night, no fedoras on the staff, no "wiseguy" specials. Placeholder story for the demo.

The GoodFellas Cafe started as a coffee cart in the lobby, run by a few friends who'd worked call-centre shifts in this building for years and were tired of bad instant and a fifteen-minute walk to anything decent.

The name is a nod to the crew — a small group who look out for each other and know everyone's order. That's the whole ethos: you're a regular by day three, you get the "usual" without asking, and if you're having a rough shift the coffee's a little stronger and nobody says anything.

It's a weekday place. Open when the offices are open, closed when they're not. The goal is a good cup and a proper feed without eating your break to get it.
