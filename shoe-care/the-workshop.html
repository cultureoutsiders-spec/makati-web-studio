One avenue, since 1971

Shoe Care has been on Don Chino Roces Avenue for over fifty years, doing one thing: making shoes, bags and leather last longer than they were meant to. The details below are a placeholder history for the demo.

How a repair moves

Drop-off to release

  1. Assessment at the counter. The item is checked, the work agreed, an estimate and a ready date written down.
  2. You get a numbered claim slip. One half is yours, one half stays on the item. No slip, no release.
  3. The bench. Sole work, stitching, dyeing and restoration are done in-house by hand and machine. Nothing is sent out.
  4. Ready check. The finished item is inspected against the ticket before it goes back on the shelf.
  5. Release. Bring your half of the slip. Pay on collection. Rush jobs settled up front.

In-house

Soles, welts, stitching, stretching, dyeing and hardware are all done at the shop, not subcontracted.

Honest calls

If a repair costs more than the item is worth, we say so. Sometimes the answer is not to do it.

Materials

Leather soles, rubber and commando compounds, full-grain leather for rebuilds, colour-matched dyes.
