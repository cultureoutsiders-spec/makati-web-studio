Your first visit

An assessment, then a plan — then a treatment

The first appointment is about 45 minutes and there's no obligation to book anything at the end of it. Here's how it goes.

Skin & goals

We look at your skin properly — under magnification, with your history and the products you use now — and you tell us what actually bothers you and what you're working toward.

Honest recommendation

What will help, what won't, and what's not worth your money. If the thing you came in asking for isn't the right call, we say so.

A course, costed

Most treatments work over a series, not a single visit. You leave with a written plan — number of sessions, spacing, and the indicative total — so there are no surprises.

Book when you're ready

Start the same day, or take the plan home and think about it. Either is fine.

Good to know

Before you come

Come with a clean face if you can, and a list of anything you're taking — including vitamins and acids.

Downtime

Varies by treatment. We'll tell you the realistic recovery for each option so you can plan around events.

Pregnancy & conditions

Tell us. Several treatments aren't suitable during pregnancy or with certain conditions, and we'll steer you to what is.
