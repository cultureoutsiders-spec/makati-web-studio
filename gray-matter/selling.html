Selling to us

We're always buying

Archive and grail pieces, streetwear to avant-garde to milsurp and Americana. If it's real, wearable and has a story, send it over.

What we want

  • Designer archive — runway, mainline, early collections
  • Japanese repro and workwear
  • Vintage militaria, deadstock preferred
  • Band and tour tees, single-stitch, pre-2000
  • Grail sneakers, worn or not, box a plus
  • Anything odd, well-made and hard to find
