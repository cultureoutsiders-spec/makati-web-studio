The process

How we grade, check & sell

The point of the record is that you don't get surprised. Here's the system behind it. Placeholder detail for the demo.

Condition scale

10

Deadstock or never worn. Tags or box where applicable.

9

Worn once or twice. No visible flaws under normal light.

8

Light use. Minor wear noted on the card — a cuff, a hem, a fade.

7

Honest wear. One or two small flaws, all photographed and listed.

6

Character. Repairs, fading or a mark that's part of the piece now.

≤5

Project pieces. Priced accordingly and described bluntly.

Authentication

What we check

  • Construction, stitching and finishing against known references
  • Labels, care tags, hardware and date codes
  • Fabric hand and weight vs. the original spec
  • Provenance — where it came from, prior owners where known

If we can't stand behind a piece, it doesn't go in the room.
