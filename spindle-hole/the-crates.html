How we grade the used bin
M / Mint
Sealed, unplayed.
NM / Near Mint
Looks unplayed, no marks you can feel, quiet surface.
VG+
Light cosmetic wear, a few faint marks, plays with almost no noise.
VG
Visible marks and some surface noise between tracks, but it plays through without skipping.
Sleeve
Graded separately from the vinyl and noted on the price tag.
