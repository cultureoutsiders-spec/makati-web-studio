How the room works

Using it

The room is free and there is no need to book for a single record. Ask at the counter and someone will set you up. A few house rules:

  • Records from the crates — play any of them here before you buy, sealed copies excepted.
  • Your own records — welcome, we just check the stylus first.
  • Hold the room — want it to yourself for an hour with a few records? Message ahead and we will set aside a slot.
The setup
  • Belt-drive deck, aligned weekly
  • Valve integrated amp
  • Stand-mount monitors + a headphone feed
  • Seats two, comfortably one
