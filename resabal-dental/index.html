Common questions

Good to know before you book

Do you see very young children?

Yes. We recommend a first visit by around age one, or when the first teeth come in. Early visits are short, friendly, and mostly about getting comfortable.

Do you take HMO or insurance?

Coverage varies by provider and plan. Tell us your HMO when you book and we’ll confirm what’s covered and what needs a letter of authorisation. Accepted providers to be confirmed by the clinic.

How much is a check-up and cleaning?

An adult exam and cleaning is quoted before anything starts, and children’s visits are priced separately. Current fees to be confirmed by the clinic.

Is parking available?

Centuria Medical Makati has paid parking on site, with lift access straight to the clinic floor.

Can the whole family be seen the same day?

Often, yes — tell us how many people and their ages when you book and we’ll block enough time.
