Services

Everything a family needs, in one clinic

Children and adults, routine and specialist — kept on one set of records so nobody repeats their history twice.

Pediatric

Children’s dentistry

First visits, cleanings, fluoride and sealants, and gentle cavity treatment. We work at the child’s pace — show-tell-do, lots of encouragement, and a parent in the room whenever it helps.

  • First dental visit (recommended by age 1)
  • Cleaning, fluoride, sealants
  • Fillings, pulp treatment, stainless-steel crowns
  • Habit counselling (thumb-sucking, bottle, diet)
Orthodontics

Braces & clear aligners

Assessment for children, teens and adults. We’ll explain what’s driving the bite or crowding, the options to fix it, and a realistic timeline and cost before you commit.

  • Metal and ceramic braces
  • Clear aligners
  • Early (interceptive) treatment for children
  • Retainers and post-treatment care
General

Check-ups, cleaning & repairs

The routine care that keeps small problems small: exams, scaling and polishing, tooth-coloured fillings, extractions, and gum treatment.

Esthetic

Cosmetic dentistry

Whitening, veneers and bonding to even out chips, gaps and staining — planned so the result still looks like you.
