Services

Everything a family needs,
in one clinic

From a toddler's first check-up to adult braces to a chipped tooth before a wedding. Here's what we handle.

Pediatric dentistry

Gentle, from the first tooth

  • · First visits and "happy visits" to build trust before any treatment
  • · Cleanings, fluoride, and sealants to prevent decay
  • · Fillings and pulp treatment done at a child's pace
  • · Advice on thumb-sucking, bottle habits and diet
  • · Early orthodontic assessment so problems are caught in time

Orthodontics

Braces & clear aligners

  • · Full assessment with x-rays and a written treatment plan
  • · Traditional metal and ceramic braces
  • · Clear aligners for suitable cases
  • · Costs and timeline agreed before treatment starts
  • · Retainers and follow-through after the braces come off

General & esthetic

Everyday care and repairs

  • · Check-ups & cleaning
  • · Tooth-coloured fillings
  • · Root canal treatment
  • · Extractions
  • · Crowns & bridges
  • · Teeth whitening
  • · Veneers
  • · Dentures
