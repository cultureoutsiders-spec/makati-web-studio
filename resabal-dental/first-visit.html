Your first visit

Know exactly what happens before you come in

No mystery, no pressure. Here’s the whole process, what to bring, and how it works for children.

The four steps

1

Get in touch

Send the booking form or call. We offer times and answer any questions first.

2

Health history

A short medical & dental form. Fill it in ahead or on arrival — bring your ID and HMO card.

3

Exam & cleaning

A full check of teeth, gums and bite, gentle scaling and polishing, and photos or an x-ray only if needed.

4

Plan & costs

We walk through findings, options and prices. You decide what happens next — nothing is done that day without your go-ahead.

Bring with you

What to prepare

  • A valid ID
  • Your HMO / insurance card, if you have one
  • Any recent dental x-rays or records from another clinic
  • A list of medicines you take, and any allergies
  • For children: their milk-teeth history if you have it, and a favourite toy

Costs

How pricing works

You get a written estimate after the exam, before any treatment. Check-ups and cleanings are quoted per person; specialist work (braces, crowns) has its own plan and payment schedule.

Current fees and accepted HMOs to be confirmed by the clinic.

Bringing a child

A first appointment that isn’t scary

We keep first visits for children short and low-pressure. The dentist explains each tool in kid-language, lets them touch it, then counts teeth with a mirror. If everything’s comfortable we do a gentle clean; if not, we stop and try again next time.

  • Best time is mid-morning, after a light meal — not a tired afternoon
  • Stay positive at home: avoid words like “hurt”, “needle” or “drill”
  • You’re welcome in the room the whole time
  • We book a little extra time so nobody feels rushed
