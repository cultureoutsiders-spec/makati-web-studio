Your first visit

Nobody's in a hurry,
including us

Whether it's a four-year-old's first check-up or an adult who hasn't been in a decade, the first visit is mostly about getting comfortable and getting a clear picture. Here's how it goes.

Step by step

A first appointment

Say hello

We go through your (or your child's) history, any pain or concerns, and what you're hoping to sort out. For kids, we let them look around and hold the mirror first.

A gentle look

A careful examination and, if needed, digital x-rays — quick and low-dose. Nothing invasive on a first visit unless you're in pain and want it dealt with.

The plain version

We show you what we see and explain what it means, in normal words. If treatment is needed, you get the options, the order to do them in, and the cost.

A clean, if there's time

Often we can do a cleaning in the same visit. If not, we book it in.

Book the next one

Follow-ups are scheduled before you leave, not left to "give us a call."

Bring with you

A valid ID, any previous x-rays or dental records if you have them, and a list of medications. For kids, a comfort toy is welcome.

If you're anxious

Tell us when you book. We can go slower, explain more, take breaks, and agree a stop signal. It genuinely helps to say it out loud.

For young children

Morning slots are best — they're fresher. Keep your own description of the visit positive and simple. We'll handle the rest.

Payment

We'll quote before treatment. Ask us about instalment options for orthodontics and larger work.

Ready when you are

Book a first visit and tell us who's coming.
