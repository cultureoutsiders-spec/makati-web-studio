Services

Four disciplines, one team

A child might need one of these, or a couple working together. Because everyone shares the same records and talks to each other, the plan stays joined-up.

Speech & language therapy

Understanding, talking, and being understood

Late talking, unclear speech, stuttering, trouble following instructions or holding a conversation — plus feeding and swallowing concerns in young children.

  • Few or no words by 18–24 months
  • Speech unclear to others past age 3
  • Doesn't respond to their name or follow simple directions
  • Frustration around communicating

A session: ~45 minutes, mostly play, with a home carry-over plan and a review with you every few weeks.

Occupational therapy

The everyday skills of being a kid

Handwriting, dressing, utensils and fine-motor tasks; sensitivity to sound, texture or movement (or constant seeking of it); attention, transitions and self-regulation.

  • Avoids or tires quickly at drawing, cutting, buttons, laces
  • Strong reactions to noise, tags, textures, messy play
  • Always moving, crashing, seeking pressure
  • Hard time settling or switching tasks

A session: a sensory warm-up, a fine-motor or self-care goal, and a regulation strategy — with a short handover for home and school.

Physical therapy

Movement, strength and balance

Delayed milestones, low or high tone, poor coordination or balance, toe-walking, or recovery after an injury or surgery.

  • Milestones noticeably behind (not walking by ~18 months)
  • Frequent falls, clumsiness, fear of playground equipment
  • Toe-walking most of the time past age 2
  • Tires much faster than peers in active play

A session: play-based movement work on one or two goals, with a short home exercise programme.

SPED tutorial

Learning support, at the child's pace

One-on-one tutorial for children with additional learning needs — readiness skills, reading and writing, numeracy, and school-work support on an individualised plan.

  • Falling behind class work and losing confidence
  • Reading, writing or number skills not clicking with usual methods
  • Needs repetition and structure to hold new skills
  • School has recommended additional support

A session: a focused block on one or two targets in small steps, with a note to you (and, with permission, the school).
