Your first visit

An assessment, before anything else

No therapy is booked until a therapist has met your child and heard your concerns. The first visit is where you get a clear picture and a realistic plan.

The four steps

Get in touch

Email or message us your child's age and what you're noticing. We'll offer assessment slots and answer questions.

Intake & history

A short form plus a conversation — development, health, school history, and what you'd most like to change.

The assessment

Play-based, with you in the room. 45–60 minutes; for younger children we sometimes split it across two shorter visits.

Findings & plan

What we saw, whether therapy is recommended, how often, and goals for the first few months. If it's “watch and review,” we'll say so.

Bring with you

What to prepare

  • Your child's health and vaccination record
  • Any past reports — developmental screening, hearing/vision, school observations
  • Your HMO / insurance card, if you have one
  • A comfort item and a snack for younger children
  • A few notes on what you're worried about and when it started

For parents

Helping your child settle

  • Book a time your child is usually alert — mid-morning, after a light meal.
  • Keep it low-key at home: “we're going to play some games with a nice teacher.”
  • You'll be in the room the whole time — the therapist will guide you.
  • It's fine if your child is shy or has an off day; we work with what we see.
