Services

Sessions

Most people come for the full integrated session. Each part is also available on its own if that's what you need.

The PhysioSpace session — the whole thing

Assessment, hands-on manual therapy, corrective movement, and a breath-and-sound close. 60–90 minutes, one practitioner. First visit runs longer.

Manual physical therapy

Joint mobilisation, soft-tissue and fascial release, and dry needling where indicated. For a specific area or a flare-up. 45–60 minutes.

Movement & yoga

One-on-one corrective and yoga-based movement — mobility, control and strength for the pattern behind your pain. Can follow a manual session or stand alone. 45–60 minutes.

Sound healing & breathwork

Guided breathing and singing-bowl sound to down-regulate the nervous system. On its own, or added to any session. 30–45 minutes.
