Why the three together

Pain rarely sits in one place

Manual therapy releases the tissue that's guarding. Movement retrains the pattern that overloaded it. Sound and breath settle the nervous system that keeps the whole thing switched on. Treat one and the others pull it back; treat all three in a session and it lasts.
