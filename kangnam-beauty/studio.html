How we work

Korean technique, Korean pigments

Our artists are trained in Korean semi-permanent makeup — the soft, hair-light approach that reads as skin rather than tattoo. We use Korean pigments, single-use needles and blades, and we periodically bring in a visiting master artist for advanced work and training.
