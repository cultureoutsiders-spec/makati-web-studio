Treatments

Pick where you want to start

Tap a feature to see what the treatment involves. Every semi-permanent service begins with a mapping and drawing session — nothing is pigmented until you're happy with the shape.

Brows

We map your brows to your bone structure and draw the shape on with pencil first. Then either microblading (fine blade, hair-by-hair strokes for a natural fill), ombré / powder (a soft machine-shaded gradient, darker at the tail), or a combination of both. A touch-up 4–6 weeks later locks in the colour.

Session ~2–3 hrs · healing 7–10 days · lasts 1–3 years

Lip blush

A buildable wash of pigment that restores definition to the lip line and evens out patchy tone. We choose a shade close to your natural lip, warmed or cooled to taste. Heals darker for a few days, then softens to a natural flush.

Session ~2–2.5 hrs · healing 5–7 days · lasts 1–3 years

Eyeliner & lashes

Lash-line enhancement deposits pigment between the lashes for a fuller lash look with no visible line. A soft wing adds a subtle tail. We also do classic and volume lash extensions and lifts if you'd rather keep it non-permanent.

Liner session ~1.5–2 hrs · healing 5–7 days · lasts 1–3 years

Pico-laser brow removal

Old, dark or mis-shaped semi-permanent brows lightened with a pico laser. It's gentler than older removal methods, done over several short sessions spaced weeks apart, so the skin recovers between visits.

Session ~20–30 min · 3+ sessions typical

Facials & acne

Treatment facials and acne care to keep skin calm and even — useful before any PMU so we're working on a good surface, and worth keeping up afterwards.

Session ~45–75 min

Colour refresh

Semi-permanent means it fades. A yearly (or 18-monthly) refresh tops up the colour and adjusts the shape if your face or your taste has moved on. Cheaper than a first session.

Session ~1–2 hrs

Good to know

Before you book

Consultation first

We'd rather talk shape and expectations before your appointment. Bring reference photos.

Plan your week

Brows and lips flake and lighten for about a week. Don't book right before a big event.

Not for everyone

Pregnancy, certain skin conditions and some medications mean we'll ask you to wait. We'll tell you honestly.
