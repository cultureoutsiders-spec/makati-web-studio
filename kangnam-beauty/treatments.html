The treatments

Every result is mapped, not stencilled

Whatever you book, the first appointment is mostly conversation and drawing. Nothing permanent happens until the shape on your face is one you love.

Brows — ombré, microblade, combination

From ₱8,000 · 2–2.5 hrs · touch-up at 4–8 weeks included

Microblading lays fine hair-like strokes for a natural, defined look. Ombré (powder) shading gives a soft, filled-in “brow makeup” finish that lasts better on oily skin. Many clients get a combination. We map the shape to your features and existing hair, draw it on, and only start once you approve it.

Healing: flaky and a little dark for about a week, softening over 4–6 weeks to the true colour.

Lip blush

From ₱12,000 · 2–3 hrs · touch-up at 6–8 weeks included

A sheer, buildable colour that restores definition to the lip border and evens out patchy tone. It reads as a natural flush, not lipstick, and makes bare lips look healthier. Colour is chosen to your undertone and heals ~30–50% softer than day one.

Lash-line eyeliner

From ₱9,000 · 1.5–2 hrs · touch-up included

A tightline enhancement makes the lashes look denser without an obvious line; a soft wing adds a defined everyday shape. Either way it doesn’t smudge, transfer or need re-doing.

Pico-laser brow removal

From ₱2,500 / session · 15–30 min · spaced 4–6 weeks apart

For old, dark, or mis-shaped permanent makeup. Pico-second pulses break the pigment down gently so the body clears it over several sessions. Far more comfortable than older laser methods, and it lets us re-do the shape cleanly afterwards.

Lashes

Classic and volume extensions, plus lifts for a low-maintenance curl. Booked separately from PMU.

Skin

Acne treatments and facials to keep skin calm before and between permanent-makeup appointments.
