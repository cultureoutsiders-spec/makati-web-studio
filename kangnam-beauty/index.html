The menu, in short

What we do

Brows

Microblading for hair-stroke detail, ombré / powder shading for a soft-makeup finish, or a combination. Mapped and drawn with you before any pigment.

Lip blush

A wash of colour that brings back definition and evens out tone — a "your lips but better" flush, not lipstick.

Eyeliner

A fine lash-line enhancement or a soft wing. No more smudging, no more re-doing it in the car.

Brow removal

Old or mis-shaped PMU lightened with pico laser — gentle, comfortable, over a few sessions.

Lashes

Classic and volume lash extensions and lifts.

Skin

Acne treatments and facials to keep the canvas in good shape.
