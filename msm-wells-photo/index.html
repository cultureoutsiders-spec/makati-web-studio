What we do

FR. 01

Develop

C-41, E-6 and true black & white, processed in-house daily. Push and pull on request.

FR. 02

Scan

High-resolution scans to JPEG or TIFF, colour-corrected frame by frame. Choose web or print size.

FR. 03

Cross-process

Slide in C-41, colour neg in E-6 — on purpose. Tell us the look you're after.

FR. 04

Print

Machine prints in standard sizes, and hand darkroom prints for the ones that matter.

FR. 05

Frame

Custom framing for prints, done alongside so you leave with something on the wall.

FR. 06

Mail-in

Send rolls by courier from anywhere in PH. We scan, upload and post the negatives back.

The honest bit

We tell you what a roll's worth doing

Underexposed, expired, water-damaged, shot at the wrong ISO — we've seen it. If a roll isn't going to give you anything, we say so before we charge you to scan a strip of black. If it's a keeper, we'll spend the time on it.
