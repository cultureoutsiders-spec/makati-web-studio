How it works

A roll, frame by frame

Same six steps whether you walk it in off EDSA or mail it from the provinces.

Hand it over

Drop-off at the lab, or courier it in a rigid box with your details inside.

Develop

Into the right chemistry — C-41, B&W or the E-6 run — with any push/pull you asked for.

Scan

Frame by frame, colour and density set per shot, to the size you chose.

Review

We flag blank or unusable rolls before charging for scans, and tell you which frames are the ones.

Prints / frame

Optional — machine prints, hand darkroom prints, or a finished frame.

Back to you

Scans by link or drive; negatives sleeved and returned, or posted back for mail-in.

Mail-in

From anywhere in PH

  • Fill the drop-off form so we know what's coming
  • Pack rolls snug in a rigid box; note your name + order inside
  • Send to the lab address by your courier of choice
  • Pay on confirmation; scans sent by link; negatives posted back

Common questions

How long, really?

Colour 35mm is usually 2–3 days. B&W and 120 a bit more. Slide waits for the weekly E-6 run.

Expired film?

Bring it. Tell us roughly how old and how it was stored — it changes how we develop it.

Do you sell film?

A small stock of the usual stocks, fridge-kept. Ask when you drop off.
