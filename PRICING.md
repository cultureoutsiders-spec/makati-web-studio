| Item | Cost |
|---|---|
| Hosting (Cloudflare Pages / GitHub Pages / Netlify) | ₱0 |

## Best way to position a ONE-OFF sale (no retainer)

This is the model to lead with for these specific budget clients. Filipino SME owners are
subscription-averse and respond well to ownership. The argument:

1. **"You own it, like your signage or your espresso machine."** Pay once, it's yours, no
   monthly bill. Contrast openly with Wix / Squarespace / agency retainers that stop working
   the day you stop paying.

2. **Hand over the keys so "lock-in" is a non-issue.** Bundle the domain setup, a year of
   hosting, a training call and a written how-to. The fear of being trapped with a vendor is
   what stalls the whole decision — remove it and the "yes" gets easier.

3. **It's cheap next to what it earns or saves.**
   - Artina / MPT / The Makati Haven: one month of OTA commission (Agoda / Booking take 15–25%
     of every reservation) usually exceeds ₱24,000. The site pays for itself the first month it
     shifts a few bookings direct.
   - 851 Bike Park / COREX: "one extra customer a week covers this in about two months."

4. **Founding-client pricing, with a real reason.** "I'm doing the first few Makati businesses
   at ₱24,000 to build my portfolio. After that it's ₱40,000." The reason-why keeps the low
   price from cheapening the work or anchoring future clients low.

5. **Show, don't pitch.** They have already seen a finished website with their name on it. The
   close is "it's done — do you want it?", not "let me build you something."

6. **De-risk the money.** 50% to start, the balance only once it's live and they're happy.

7. **Mention the monthly once, unbundled.** "Or if you'd rather not touch it, I keep it running
   and make changes for ₱1,800/mo — optional, most people self-manage after the handover call."
   Offered, never assumed.

## Facts for your pitch (all true of what was built)

- **Mobile-responsive, built mobile-first.** On a phone the menu becomes a tap-to-open drawer,
  multi-column sections stack to one column, and text sizes scale down. Same design, adapted —
  not a separate cut-down version, and no sideways scrolling. Test any live URL on your phone.
- **5 real pages each**, plus a custom 404 page.
- **No monthly platform fee to anyone.** Plain HTML/CSS/JS; hosts free on Cloudflare Pages,
  GitHub Pages or Netlify. The only unavoidable cost is the domain (~₱1,000/yr).
- **Basic SEO built in:** proper page titles and descriptions, Open Graph tags for link
  previews, `LocalBusiness` / `Hotel` structured data, a sitemap.
- **Working enquiry / booking forms** that email the owner (via Web3Forms — free).
- **Fast:** no heavy frameworks, images are the only real weight.
- **Portable:** the whole site is one folder the client can move to any host.
