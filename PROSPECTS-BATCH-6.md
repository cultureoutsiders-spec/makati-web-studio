## The five

### 1. Bibio — natural / low-intervention wine bar
- **Where:** 5659 Don Pedro Street, Barangay Poblacion, Makati
- **Presence:** Instagram `@bibio.mnl` + press write-ups. **No website** (search engines mangle
  the IG handle `bibio.mnl` into a fake "bibio.mnl" URL — there is no real domain).
- **Contact:** +63 917 106 1265 · Instagram DM. Open Mon–Thu 5pm–12am, Fri–Sat 5pm–1am, closed
  Sun. Walk-ins welcome; kitchen partners with guest chefs.
- **Pitch angle:** natural wine needs explaining before someone orders — what "low-intervention"
  means, what's open by the glass this week, what the fermented / cured small plates are. A site
  with a rotating by-the-glass list, the food menu, the "what is natural wine" primer and a
  table-request form does that. Distinct from Ambient (batch 2, a bar + events venue) — this is a
  wine-led room with a point of view.
- **Template:** a warm editorial one-to-two-pager — rotating list + menu + primer + reservation
  form (its own look: label typography, hand-written vintages, a stained-paper palette).

### 2. Dan's Bike Shop — road & mountain bike shop + workshop
- **Where:** W13-A La Fuerza Plaza Compound, 2241 Don Chino Roces Avenue, Makati
- **Presence:** Facebook `/DansBikeShopPH` + manufacturer dealer-locators (Trek, Specialized,
  Fox, La Sportiva, On). **No own website.** A long-running, well-regarded shop — sales, service
  and parts.
- **Contact:** (02) 8553 4919 · Facebook Messenger.
- **Pitch angle:** a serious bike shop is a destination — people check brands carried, service
  turnaround, fitting, and whether a part is in stock before making the trip down Chino Roces.
  All of that is in Facebook posts. A site with the brand wall, a service menu + booking, a
  bike-fit page and a parts/stock enquiry is a straight upgrade. (Slightly more established than a
  typical prospect — it's a Trek/Specialized concept-store dealer — but still has no site of its
  own.)
- **Template:** services + brand/stock + service-booking form (adapts the 851 Bike Park
  motorcycle-dealer pattern from batch 1 to bicycles, lighter and more workshop-forward).

### 3. The Golden Fur Makati — pet grooming, vet & pet hotel
- **Where:** Unit 2A, Paseo Tesoro Building, 822 Arnaiz Ave., Barangay San Lorenzo, Makati
- **Presence:** Facebook `TheGoldenFurMakati` + a separate `TGFvetcenterMakati` page + TikTok
  `@thegoldenfurph`. Listed on PetBacker / Pickaroo / ClinicFinderPH marketplaces. **No own
  website.** Grooming + veterinary + boarding, award-cited locally.
- **Contact:** Facebook / Instagram / TikTok DM.
- **Pitch angle:** pet parents pick a groomer/vet on trust and detail — the grooming packages by
  size and coat, the vet services and hours, the boarding rates and what a day looks like, the
  team. None of that is legible in a feed. A site with a services + pricing grid, a boarding
  page, the team, and a booking form is exactly what's missing.
- **Template:** services + pricing + team + booking (the Resabal / clinic pattern from batch 2,
  warmed up for pets — rounded, friendly, photo-led).

### 4. The GoodFellas Cafe — weekday office-district cafe
- **Where:** Ground Floor, 45 Wisma Cyberhub, Sen. Gil Puyat Ave., Barangay Palanan, Makati
- **Presence:** Facebook page only (`goodfellascafe.shop` is an unrelated business). **No
  website.** Open Mon–Fri 9am–6pm — a cafe built around the BPO / office crowd in the building
  and the block.
- **Contact:** **`tgfc917.makati@gmail.com`** · +63 927 432 0888 · Facebook Messenger.
  **Email-reachable** — the only one in batches 5–6.
- **Pitch angle:** a weekday cafe serving an office district lives on "what's the menu, do you do
  pickup/catering for a meeting, where exactly is Wisma Cyberhub." A tight site with the menu,
  a meeting-catering / bulk-coffee order form, hours, and a clear map does the job. Different
  brief from Koffle (batch 3, a hidden storybook cafe) — this one is brisk, functional, made for
  a 20-minute lunch break.
- **Template:** menu + pickup / catering order form + find-us (a clean, quick, weekday-morning
  identity — no whimsy, good wayfinding).

### 5. Hoshi House Matcha Cafe — matcha slow-bar
- **Where:** 1738 Dian St., Barangay Palanan, Makati (11am–9pm)
- **Presence:** Instagram `@hoshihouse.cafe` (~3.4K) + TikTok. **No website, no listed phone.**
  Very new (opened 2025). Single-cultivar Okumidori matcha, medium-roast hojicha, dango; a
  playful K-pop-fandom streak.
- **Contact:** Instagram DM only.
- **Pitch angle:** a specialty matcha bar is a ritual and a story — the cultivar, the grades, the
  drink list, the dessert pairings, the tiny room. Right now that's a stack of IG reels. A calm,
  precise site with the menu, the "about our matcha" page, hours and a pre-order form fits the
  slow-bar ethos and helps a brand-new place get found.
- **Template:** menu + provenance/story + visit + pre-order form (a serene, Japanese-tea-room
  identity — green + paper + a single vertical brushstroke; distinct from every other F&B site so
  far).
