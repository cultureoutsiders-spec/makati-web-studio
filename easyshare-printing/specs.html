File checklist

Send us a print-ready file

  • PDF/X or high-res PDF; DWG/DXF for plans; AI/EPS for vector art
  • CMYK colour, not RGB, for anything colour-critical
  • 3mm bleed + crop marks on bordered / full-bleed work
  • Fonts embedded or outlined
  • Images 300dpi at final size (150dpi is fine for large-format viewed at distance)

Not sure? Send what you have — we preflight before we print.
