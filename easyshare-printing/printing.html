What we print

The full list

If it's on paper, board, film or vinyl, it's probably here. Not sure how to spec something? Put it in the quote form in plain words and we'll translate it.

Plan & CAD plotting

For drawings, not documents

  • A0 / A1 laser CAD plotting, mono & colour
  • Odourless blue-line (diazo replacement) printing
  • Large-format scanning to PDF / TIFF
  • Tracing paper, drafting film, bond & presentation stocks
  • Plan folding to A4 & binding into sets
  • Mounting on foam board for reviews
Business print

The office run

  • Business cards — matte, gloss, soft-touch, spot UV
  • Letterheads, envelopes, NCR forms
  • Brochures, leaflets, folded flyers
  • Booklets & company profiles, saddle-stitched or bound
  • Annual reports & proposals, short-run
  • Presentation folders with pockets
Large format

Seen from across the room

  • Tarpaulins & streamers, eyelets or pole pockets
  • Roll-up banners, X-banners, tabletop standees
  • Posters to A0+, indoor & weatherproof
  • Panel & sintra mounting, contour cutting
  • Backdrops & step-and-repeat walls
  • Vehicle & window stickers
Bindery & finishing

Turning a stack into a piece

  • Ring, comb, spiral & perfect binding
  • Lamination — gloss, matte, anti-scratch
  • Numbering, perforation, scoring & folding
  • Guillotine trimming to size
  • PVC ID printing with lanyards
  • Padding (notepads), stapling, collation
