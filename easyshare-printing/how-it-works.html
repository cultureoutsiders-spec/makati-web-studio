How it works

Five steps, no surprises

Every job runs the same way, whether it's one plot or a thousand booklets. The proof step is where you catch anything before it costs you a reprint.

Send the file

Email it, message it, or bring it on a drive. Tell us size, stock, quantity and when you need it.

We preflight

We check colour space, resolution, bleed and fonts, and flag anything that'll print wrong.

You approve a proof

A digital proof, or a printed sample for colour-critical work. Nothing runs without your yes.

Print & finish

Run, trim, laminate, fold, bind — whatever the job needs, done in-house.

Collect or deliver

Pick up at the shop, or we send it by courier or messenger around Makati.

Turnaround

How fast is fast

  • CAD plots & business cards — same day, often within hours
  • Brochures & flyers, short run — next day
  • Booklets & bound reports — 1–3 days by quantity
  • Large format & standees — same or next day
  • Rush jobs — tell us the deadline, we'll tell you if it's possible

For studios & offices

Regular work

If you print with us often — a design studio doing weekly plots, an office running monthly reports — ask about a running account: agreed rates, a standing pickup time, and one monthly invoice instead of a receipt every visit.
